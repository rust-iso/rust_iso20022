not introduce a high-level field-copying message DTO.

## Implemented surface

- `MessageIdentifier` and `MessageVersion` preserve fixed-width wire identity
  while providing numeric version ordering;
- `ParsedMessage` owns one generated `AnyMessage`; `MessageRef` borrows it;
- 1,130 feature-gated enum variants, typed borrow accessors, typed ownership
  recovery methods, and `MxMessage` implementations are generated from schema
  descriptor coverage;
- XML/JSON serialization delegates to the concrete generated value;
- unknown identity, namespace/root mismatch, disabled model feature, generated
  deserialization, and serialization failures are typed without changing the
  existing exhaustive `Error` enum;
- non-`Document` roots are supported: head `AppHdr` resolves to
  `BusinessApplicationHeaderV04`, and the generated header roots remain directly
  accessible;
- enveloped pacs.008 parsing is demonstrated by the runnable
  `inspect_message` example and recovers
  `generated::pacs::pacs_008_001_08::Document` by reference.

The compatibility APIs keep their signatures. The new parser shares the legacy
`detect`, `from_xml`, and `to_xml` core, while `parse_as`, `MxMessage`, `MxId`,
and `Error` remain unchanged. The downstream default fixture still externally
implements `MxMessage` and exhaustively matches `Error`; its `model-pacs` build
uses only pacs generated types. Parser resource limits remain explicitly
scheduled for WP-028 rather than being claimed by this WP.
