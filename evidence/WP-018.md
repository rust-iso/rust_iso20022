## Public release identity

Public EPC pages reviewed on 2026-09-28 identify these current effective source
sets:

- SCT: EPC125-05 2025 rulebook v1.1, EPC132-08 C2PSP IG 2025 v1.0, and
  EPC115-06 Inter-PSP IG 2025 v1.0.
- SCT Inst: EPC004-16 2025 rulebook v1.1, EPC121-16 C2PSP IG 2025 v1.0, and
  EPC122-16 Inter-PSP IG 2025 v1.0.
- Both rulebooks took effect on 2025-10-05 and are identified by EPC as current
  through 2027-11-21.
- EPC008-26 and EPC009-26 are consultation/change-request documents and are not
  effective releases.

The independent-implementation/distribution decision and exact official URLs
are recorded in `profiles/sepa/source-review.json`. No protected document text
or third-party message model was copied.

## Implemented framework

`src/profiles/sepa/mod.rs` adds typed scheme, source-role, publication-status,
date, edition, digest, and release eligibility concepts. It:

- distinguishes rulebooks from C2PSP and Inter-PSP guidelines;
- rejects consultation and merely published documents as effective releases;
- rejects SCT/SCT Inst source mixing;
- requires at least one separately identified implementation guideline;
- exposes missing source digests and returns `is_production_ready() == false`
  until all exact source bytes are pinned;
- contains no message DTO and does not alter the canonical generated structs.

`src/profiles/provisional.rs` adds an opt-in SCT developer rule set for IBAN and
currency syntax. It is deliberately labelled `provisional` and is not a
substitute for the EPC rulebook or implementation guidelines.

## Remaining T093 gate

The shell environment could not download the official PDF/ZIP bytes because its
network proxy endpoint was unavailable. The web sources established identities,
status, dates, and URLs, but do not expose raw bytes from which an honest SHA-256
can be calculated. `source-review.json` therefore uses `sha256: null`, and no
production `profiles/sepa/source-manifest.json` exists yet.

T093 and WP-018 remain open until exact official bytes are obtained, hashed, and
validated against `profiles/manifest.schema.json`. Consequently WP-019 and
WP-020 may develop only independently evidenced rules; neither may claim a
complete production SEPA rule pack yet.
